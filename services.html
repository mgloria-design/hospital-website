Our services

Inpatient and outpatient care, 24 hours a day, in Mutalia.

In an emergencyWe are open 24 hours. Do not wait for a booking. Come straight to the hospital, or call +254 713 187 220.

Comprehensive emergency care

Our emergency team is available 24 hours a day for accidents, acute illness and other urgent cases.

  • Triage on arrival
  • Assessment and treatment
  • Admission to a ward when needed

Diagnostic tests

Tests that help your doctor find the cause of your symptoms and decide on the right treatment.

  • Tests ordered by your doctor
  • Results reviewed with you
  • Results used to plan treatment

Surgical procedures

Surgery carried out by our surgical team, with preparation before and care afterwards.

  • Consultation before surgery
  • Surgery by our surgical team
  • Recovery care on the ward

Maternity care

Support for mothers and babies through pregnancy, delivery and the period after birth.

  • Antenatal care
  • Delivery care
  • Postnatal care for mother and baby

Specialist consultations

See a specialist for a focused assessment, a second opinion or ongoing treatment.

  • Specialist assessment
  • Treatment plans
  • Follow-up visits

How you can use our services

Outpatient or inpatient

Outpatient care

Visit us for a consultation, tests or a specialist appointment and go home the same day.

Inpatient care

If your condition needs it, we admit you to a ward and look after you until you are ready to go home.

Insurance accepted

We accept insurance. Bring your card to reception when you arrive.
