Admissions and patient registration

What happens when you arrive, and what to bring so that registration is quick. Admissions are open 24 hours.

At reception

We register you, then guide you

Every patient starts at reception. Our team records your details, opens your file and sends you to triage or the right clinic. In an emergency, you are seen first and registered afterwards.

How admission works

From the front desk to the ward

  1. 1

    Arrive at reception

    Tell the receptionist why you came. Emergencies go straight through.

  2. 2

    Register

    We record your details and open or find your patient file.

  3. 3

    Triage

    A nurse checks your vital signs and decides how urgent your case is.

  4. 4

    See the clinician

    Your doctor examines you and agrees a plan, including any tests.

  5. 5

    Admission or discharge

    If you need to stay, we admit you to a ward. Otherwise you go home with your treatment plan.

Before you come

What to bring

Having these ready helps reception register you in a few minutes. We accept insurance.

  • IdentificationYour national ID or passport. For a child, a birth certificate or clinic card.
  • Insurance cardWe accept insurance. Show your card at reception when you register.
  • Referral letter and past recordsAny letters, test results or discharge notes from another facility.
  • Your medicinesBring what you take now, or a list of names and doses.
